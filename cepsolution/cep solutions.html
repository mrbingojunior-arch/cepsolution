About CEP Solution

CEP Solution (Community Empowering Plan) is a community-based microfinance organisation focused on promoting financial discipline through savings and providing affordable loans to individuals and groups.

Registration & Savings

Registration Fee

One-time membership fee: KES 400

Savings

Minimum savings: KES 400

Maximum savings: Member’s choice

Savings determine loan eligibility

Loan Products

First Loan (Starter Loan)

Amount: KES 100 – 3,000

Interest: 10%

Repayment period: 1 month

Second Loan (Growth Loan)

Loan limit: 3 × Savings

Range: KES 5,000 – 900,000

Interest: 2%

Repayment: 15 – 30 months

Emergency / Advance Loan

Quick access for urgent needs

Table Banking (Pesa ya Mezani)

Group-based saving and lending system
